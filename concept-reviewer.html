Wording worth getting right

  • Regression does not require a linear relationship. Trees and kernel methods can model nonlinear numerical targets.
  • R² can be negative. Lasso is L1; ridge is L2. The main slides contain conflicting wording on these points.
  • The 37% OOB figure is approximate and applies to one tree’s bootstrap sample. OOB is an internal estimate, not a separate final test set.
  • The notes’ hard-vote forest illustration and scikit-learn’s probability-averaging classifier are related but not identical rules.
  • The general boosting weight-update narrative is AdaBoost-like. Gradient boosting follows loss gradients; its classification sums are not automatically probabilities.
